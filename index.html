Who we serve

Every box has two
kinds of buyer.

Scholar Box

Bronx parents of K–8 kids, household income roughly $40k–$70k, buying a monthly subscription directly.

Hustle Box

Bronx adults age 18–35 exploring a side hustle or small business, subscribing on their own.

Scholar Box

Schools, churches, and nonprofits sponsoring boxes as gifts for families in need.

Hustle Box

Workforce development orgs sponsoring boxes for participants in their programs.

How it works

Lean by design —
lowest cost, highest effort.

01

Pre-order

Payment collected before a single item is bought — zero inventory risk.

02

Source local

Bulk-bought from Bronx wholesalers and small vendors, not big-box retailers.

03

Pack by hand

No warehouse. Every box is assembled at home to start.

04

Deliver locally

Hand-delivered nearby to build trust; USPS flat-rate covers the rest.
